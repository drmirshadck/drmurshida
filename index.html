Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account or install an app?

No. You book with just your name, email and phone number, and the video call opens in your browser on this website. Just allow camera and microphone access when asked.

How do I join my video consultation?

After booking, you'll receive an email with a link to the consultation room on this website. At your appointment time, open the link, enter your name and wait — the doctor will let you in. You can also tap "Join video call" in the menu.

How long is an online consultation?

Each online video consultation is 30 minutes.

Which languages are consultations in?

Malayalam and English.

Can I get a prescription online?

Yes. Where appropriate, prescriptions and diet plans are shared with you digitally after the consultation.

Is online consultation suitable for emergencies?

No. For a medical emergency, go to the nearest hospital or call emergency services immediately.
